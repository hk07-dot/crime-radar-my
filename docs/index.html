      Police districts nearby

        Official figures are annual totals per police district from the Royal Malaysia Police, published on data.gov.my (CC BY 4.0). They count crimes where a conviction was secured, not every report. News and social items are placed at neighbourhood level from the article text and can be wrong. Hollow pins marked town-level only give the town where the story was filed. Social posts are unverified. The red shading spreads each police district's annual case count around its HQ and ranks areas nationally: "very high" is the densest ground that together holds 10% of all cases in Malaysia. It shows case counts, not crime per person, and can't tell one street from the next. In an emergency call 999.
